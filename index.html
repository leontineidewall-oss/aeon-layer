02 — THE TRENCHES HAVE EYES

Three ways your trail costs you.

None of this needs hacking. It's all public data, read by software that never sleeps.

Copied

Copy-bots follow wallets that win. The moment you buy, they buy behind you, and your edge is gone before your transaction settles.

Sandwiched

Bots see your swap before it lands, buy first and sell into you. You get a worse price, and they keep the difference.

Labeled

Trackers tag wallets as dev, sniper, whale or insider. The label follows you forever, and everyone you trade against can read it.

03 — HOW AEON WORKS

Four steps out of sight.

AEON is a shielded layer built on encrypted computation. Funds go in under one name and come out under another, with nothing on-chain to tie them together.

01 · SHIELD
01

Shield

Deposit SOL into a shielded balance. Amounts and ownership are encrypted, so trackers watching the pool see a deposit, not a person.

07 — QUESTIONS

Asked in the dark.

Is AEON a mixer?

It's a shielded balance, which is close. The difference is that balances stay encrypted while they're in the layer, so you can wait, split and withdraw on your own schedule instead of sending a fixed amount through a fixed pipe.

Does AEON hold my funds?

It's designed so it can't. Funds sit in an on-chain program, and only your key can move them. No team wallet can spend them.

What tech is it built on?

Arcium's encrypted compute network handles the private balances. Trades from fresh wallets route through Jupiter or the pump.fun bonding curve like any other wallet.

Is privacy legal?

Financial privacy is normal: your bank balance isn't public. AEON screens deposits against sanctions lists and doesn't serve flagged wallets. Check the rules where you live.

What does the scanner do with my address?

It reads public data from a Solana RPC and shows it to you. Nothing is stored, and no wallet connection is needed.

When can I use the layer?

When the status above says live. We'd rather ship it late than ship it leaky.
